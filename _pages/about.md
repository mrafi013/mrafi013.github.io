I am a third-year PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC Riverside,
working in [Hua's Lab](https://www.ece.ucr.edu/) under the supervision of
[Professor Yingbo Hua](https://profiles.ucr.edu/app/home/profile/yhua).
My research is funded by the U.S. Department of Defense (AFRL/ARL).

My work sits at the intersection of **physical-layer security**, **probabilistic constellation shaping**,
and **machine learning for wireless systems**. Current research threads include:

- **STEEP protocol** — information-theoretic security for finite constellations (PSK, APSK, QAM, PCS-QAM),
  covering secret-key capacity, secrecy rate, LDPC-coded BER, and stochastic eavesdropper models via PPP geometry.
- **LEO satellite anomaly detection** — conditional VAE and normalizing flow models for unsupervised anomaly
  detection on real Starlink drive-test telemetry (AUROC 0.916).
- **RIS beam selection via DANN** — domain-adversarial adaptation for reconfigurable intelligent surface
  beam selection under LOS-to-NLOS propagation shifts.

I received my M.S. in ECE from UC Riverside (GPA 3.90/4.00) and my undergraduate degree from
Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Bangladesh, where I received the Best Paper Award at EECSI 2021.
I am an IEEE Student Member and a recipient of the UC Riverside Dean's Distinguished Fellowship (2023).
